What booking earlier is worth

What these numbers are. Fares our scraper saw on travel portals, each stored with the moment we saw it. “Normal” is what the same route cost at the same notice over the last 30 days. We do not sell tickets, and a price here is indicative — an airline can move a fare between our last check and your booking.
